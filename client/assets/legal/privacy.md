# Privacy Policy

DRAFT — pending legal review. This text is a placeholder and is not final.

## What we collect
- Account: your name, email address and a hashed password.
- Check-ins: the time of each check-in, the club, and the distance between you and the club at that moment.

Your location is only read at the moment you check in. We do not track you in the background.

## Why we collect it
To build your personal map of visited clubs, show your stats, and verify that you were at the venue when you checked in.

## Who can see it
Your map and check-in history are private to you.

## Retention
Check-in data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. The final retention period is to be decided.

## Deletion and export
You can remove a check-in or delete your account in the app. You can also download a copy of your data from your profile.
